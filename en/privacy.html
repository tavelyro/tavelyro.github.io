RotaRest: Shift Sleep Planner ("the app") is published by MB Tavelyro ("we", "us"), Naujakiemio 17-14, Klaipėda LT-94205, Lithuania. Email: tavelyro@proton.me. We are the controller only for the personal data described under "If you contact us" and "Purchases through Google Play".

In short

The app works offline. Your roster, your settings and everything else the app stores stay on your phone, unless you save a backup file somewhere else yourself. It does not send your data anywhere by itself. We never receive, access or store that data, including any health-related information it may reveal. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your roster and your settings.
  • Whether Pro is active and when Google Play last confirmed it.
  • Which version of the terms of use you accepted.
  • For each purchase you start, the time you gave the EU withdrawal consent and the product.

This data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and it is excluded from Android backups and phone-to-phone transfers. It stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. To move your roster and settings to a new phone, you can back them up to a file and restore them from it (see "Backing up your roster"); Pro is restored from Google Play with Restore purchases.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your data anywhere, and it does not sell or share it.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across apps or websites, so "Do Not Track" signals have nothing to act on.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, microphone, camera or health sensors.

Permissions

The app asks for permission to show notifications, to set exact alarms and to start after the phone restarts, so that reminders arrive at the planned time. These permissions are used only on your phone.

Purchases through Google Play

Google Play handles payments, and we receive no payment details. Google processes purchase data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play which purchases you own; this check stays between your phone and Google Play.

Google shows us limited order information in our developer account, such as the order number, the product, the price and the country. We receive this order information from Google Play; we do not collect it from you directly. We use it only to manage sales, refunds and withdrawals and to meet our tax and accounting duties (legal basis: performing our contract with you and our legal obligations, GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (c)). We retain this order information for 10 years following the end of the calendar year of purchase, in accordance with applicable statutory accounting and tax retention periods.

Sharing a plan

If you choose Share plan (CSV), the app hands the plan, as CSV text, to the app you pick. What that app does with it is governed by that app's own policy.

Backing up your roster

If you choose Back up roster in Settings, the app saves your roster and your settings in a text file. You choose the file's name and where it goes, for example a folder on your phone or a storage app or service. The file holds only your roster and settings, not your Pro status or anything else the app stores. The app reads a backup file only when you choose it with Restore from a backup. We never receive the file. What happens to it is up to you and is governed by the policy of the app or service where you keep it.
