We are the controller only for the personal data described under "If you contact us", "Purchases through Google Play" and "Statistics, crash reports and reviews on Google Play".

In short

The app works offline. Your roster, your settings and the other app data listed below stay on your phone: the app never sends them to us or to anyone else. They leave your phone only if you export them or back them up yourself, and then only to the place you choose. We do not receive your roster or settings, or any health-related information they may reveal, unless you send them to us yourself, for example in a support email (see "If you contact us"). For purchases, the app works with Google Play, and the Google Play Billing library that it uses sends Google technical logs (see "Purchases through Google Play"). The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your roster and your settings.
  • Whether Pro is active and when Google Play last confirmed it.
  • Which version of the terms of use you accepted.
  • For each purchase you start, the product and the time you asked for Pro to be provided at once (the consent that affects the EU right of withdrawal).
  • The latest calendar file, if you used Add to calendar (.ics).

Android keeps this data in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a phone that has not been rooted or otherwise modified. The app excludes it from Android backups and phone-to-phone transfers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes all of it. Copies that you made yourself are not deleted with it: backup files you saved, calendar files you shared with another app and events you imported into a calendar app stay there until you delete them. To move your roster and settings to a new phone, you can back them up to a file and restore them from it (see "Backing up your roster"); Pro is restored from Google Play with Restore purchases.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your roster or settings anywhere, and it does not sell or share your personal data.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across apps or websites, so "Do Not Track" signals have nothing to act on.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, microphone, camera or health sensors.

Permissions

The app asks for permission to show notifications, to set exact alarms and to start after the phone restarts, so that reminders arrive at the planned time. These permissions are used only on your phone. The Google Play Billing library adds network access to the app; only that library uses it, for purchases and for the logs described under "Purchases through Google Play".

Purchases through Google Play

Google Play handles payments, and we receive no payment details. Google processes purchase data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play which purchases you own. The Google Play Billing library, which Google provides and the app uses for purchases, also sends Google technical logs about its purchase functions, which Google processes under its own privacy policy. These logs do not contain your roster or settings, and we do not receive them.

Google shows us limited order information in our developer account, such as the order number, the product, the price and the country. We receive this order information from Google Play; we do not collect it from you directly. We use it only to manage sales, refunds and withdrawals and to meet our tax and accounting duties (legal basis: performing our contract with you and our legal obligations, GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (c)). We keep it for as long as the accounting and tax laws of the country where we are established require, currently 10 years after the end of the calendar year of the purchase.

Statistics, crash reports and reviews on Google Play

Google processes data about installing and updating the app as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. In our developer account, Google shows us:

  • Statistics about the app, such as the number of installs by country, phone model and Android version. These are totals that do not identify you.
  • If your phone shares usage and diagnostics data with Google, reports of the app's crashes and freezes, with the phone model, the Android version, the app version, the country and technical details of the error.
  • If you review the app on Google Play, your review and the name Google shows with it. We may reply to it publicly on Google Play.

We use this information only to fix errors, to improve the app and to answer reviews (legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working app and in communicating with users, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We do not download it or keep copies of it elsewhere, except what we quote when we answer you by email, which we keep as described under "If you contact us". Google decides how long the developer account shows this information; once Google removes it there, we no longer have it. You can turn off usage and diagnostics sharing in your Google account or phone settings, and you can edit or delete your review on Google Play at any time.

Sharing a plan

If you choose Add to calendar (.ics), the app saves your shifts and the next four weeks of your plan as a calendar file in its own storage on your phone, replacing the previous one, and opens that file in your calendar app or, if there is none, shares it with an app you choose. What that app does with it is governed by that app's own policy. Events you import stay in your calendar until you delete them there.

Backing up your roster

If you choose Back up roster in Settings, the app saves your roster and your settings in a text file. You choose the file's name and where it goes, for example a folder on your phone or a storage app or service. The file holds only your roster and settings, not your Pro status or anything else the app stores. It is a plain text file and is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere only you can open. The app reads a backup file only when you choose it with Restore from a backup. We do not receive the file unless you send it to us. What happens to it is up to you and is governed by the policy of the app or service where you keep it.
